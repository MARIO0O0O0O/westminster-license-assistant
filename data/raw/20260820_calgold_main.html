California Business Permit Assistance (CalGOLD)

State & Local Permit Guidance Overview

CalGOLD (California Government Online Assessment tool) assists business owners in identifying potential local, state, and federal permit requirements for operating a business in California municipalities, including the City of Westminster (Orange County).

Common State & Regional Permits for Westminster Businesses

Depending on your business type, the following agency permits may be required in addition to a Westminster City Business License:

  • Seller's Permit (CDTFA): Required for all businesses selling or leasing tangible personal property in California. Issued by the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration.
  • Environmental Health Permit (OC Health): Food facilities, restaurants, public pools, tattoo parlors, and body art studios in Westminster require health permits from the Orange County Health Care Agency.
  • Air Quality Permit (SCAQMD): Industrial, auto body, and chemical operations may require air quality permits from the South Coast Air Quality Management District.
  • State Professional Licenses (DCA / CSLB): Contractors, cosmetologists, accountants, medical professionals, and real estate brokers must maintain active licenses with their respective California Department of Consumer Affairs boards.
