Applying for a Westminster Business License

Step 1: Check Zoning and Zoning Clearance

Before submitting a business license application for a commercial location in Westminster, verify that your proposed business activity is permitted under the City's Zoning Code. Contact the Planning Division at (714) 548-3240 for zoning clearance or Conditional Use Permit (CUP) guidance.

Step 2: Submit Application Online via HdL Portal

The City of Westminster partners with HdL Companies to process business license applications and payments online. Applicants should access the online portal at westminster.hdlgov.com to complete the application form, upload required state licenses, and pay fees.

Step 3: Required Documentation & Fees

All applications must include a Federal Employer Identification Number (FEIN) or Social Security Number (SSN), California State Board of Equalization / CDTFA Seller's Permit (if selling tangible goods), and CSLB license (for contractors).

State Fee Notice: Under California SB 1186 / AB 1379, a mandatory $4.00 state fee is added to every local business license application or renewal to fund disability access compliance and training.

Step 4: License Display Requirement

Upon approval and payment, your official City Business License certificate will be issued. The license certificate must be conspicuously displayed at the principal place of business in Westminster or kept on site for mobile contractors.
