Welcome to the City of Westminster Business License Portal

Online Business License Services

HdL Companies provides automated business license filing, renewal, and tax processing for the City of Westminster. Through this portal, business owners can apply for a new license, renew an existing license, pay outstanding balances, or report a business closure.

Getting Started & Application Requirements

To complete an application online, you will need the following information ready:

  • Ownership structure (Sole Proprietorship, LLC, Corporation, Partnership)
  • Federal Employer Identification Number (FEIN) or Taxpayer Identification Number
  • Exact business location address and square footage
  • Estimated annual gross receipts in Westminster
  • Emergency contact phone numbers
  • State professional license number (e.g. CSLB, Medical Board, Bureau of Barbering) if applicable
