Business License Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Who is required to obtain a business license in Westminster?

Under Westminster Municipal Code Title 5, every person, business, contractor, landlord, or independent professional operating, conducting, soliciting, or engaging in business within the city limits of Westminster, California is required to obtain a City Business License prior to commencing operations.

This requirement applies to in-city commercial businesses, home-based businesses, out-of-city contractors performing work inside Westminster, and residential landlords renting three (3) or more residential units, as well as commercial property landlords leasing to one (1) or more tenants.

What are the requirements for Home-Based Businesses?

All home-based businesses operating in a residential zone in Westminster must obtain a Home Occupation Permit approved by the Planning Division before a business license can be issued. The Home Occupation Permit ensures that the business activity remains secondary to the residential use of the property and does not create negative traffic, noise, or parking impacts on the neighborhood.

Do contractors need a Westminster business license?

Yes. Both in-city and out-of-city contractors performing construction, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, or general contracting work within the City of Westminster must hold a valid Westminster Business License and present an active California Contractors State License Board (CSLB) license prior to pulling permits or starting work.

What are the rules for Residential and Commercial Landlords?

Residential landlords who own and lease three (3) or more residential dwelling units within the City of Westminster are required to obtain a residential landlord business license. Commercial property landlords leasing commercial or industrial space to one (1) or more business tenants are also required to maintain a commercial landlord business license.

Are Police Permits or Conditional Use Permits (CUP) required for certain business types?

Yes. Specific business classifications—including massage establishments, firearms dealers, pawnshops, secondhand dealers, alcohol sales, cannabis operations, auto repair, and entertainment venues—require specialized clearance. Applicants must obtain Police Department permits or Planning Commission Conditional Use Permits (CUP) prior to business license approval.
