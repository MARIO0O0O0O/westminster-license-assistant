Department Contact & Office Hours

The Business License Division oversees business registration, tax administration, and compliance for all entities doing business in Westminster, CA.

Location: City Hall, 8200 Westminster Blvd., Westminster, CA 92683

Phone: (714) 548-3254

HdL Customer Support Line: (657) 622-0222

  • Landlord Licensing Compliance Verification
