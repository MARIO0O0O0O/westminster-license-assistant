Annual Business License Renewal Portal

Renewal Deadlines & Due Dates

All City of Westminster Business Licenses expire annually on December 31st. Renewal notices are mailed and emailed by HdL in November of each year. Payments are due on or before January 31st to avoid delinquent penalty charges.

Delinquent Penalty Schedule

If renewal payment is not received by January 31st, delinquent penalties accrue at a rate of 10% per month on the unpaid balance, up to a maximum penalty of 50%. Licenses unpaid after March 31st are referred to Code Enforcement for unpermitted operation.

How to Renew Online

To renew online at westminster.hdlgov.com/Renew, enter your 7-digit Business License Account Number and the secure PIN provided on your renewal notice. Review your reported gross receipts, verify contact details, and complete payment via Visa, Mastercard, or e-Check.
